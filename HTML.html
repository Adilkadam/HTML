BMW (Bayerische Motoren Werke AG) is a premier German luxury vehicle and motorcycle manufacturer founded in 1916. Headquartered in Munich, the company originally built aircraft engines before shifting to motorcycles in 1923 and automobiles in 1928. Today, the [BMW Group] owns the MINI and Rolls-Royce brands alongside BMW Motorrad. Known for precision engineering and the famous slogan "The Ultimate Driving Machine", BMW produces a diverse lineup spanning sports sedans, high-performance M models, and electric vehicles. The company stands as a major global leader in the luxury automotive market.

FREQUENTLY ASK QUESTION

What is the difference between M Sport, M Performance, and a full "M" car?

The M3 is a four-door high-performance sedan, while the M4 is its two-door coupe (or convertible) counterpart with a lower roofline and wider stance.

What kind of fuel economy or mileage do BMW M cars get?

Fuel efficiency varies heavily by model and driving style. Because they prioritize high horsepower twin-turbocharged engines (like the S58 or V8 variants), city mileage is quite low, while highway cruising is more moderate.

What does the "M" stand for in BMW M?

It stands for Motorsport. The division started in 1972 as BMW's racing branch and released its first street-legal car, the M1, in 1978.
